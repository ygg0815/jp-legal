TintStash is a single-device tool for window-film stock and cut planning. This policy describes version 1.0 of the app and these support pages.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

In brief

TintStash does not automatically send your workshop records to the developer. There is no developer account, developer server, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or tracking across other apps and websites. Stock and cut calculations run on your device.

Apple handles optional in-app purchases. You choose whether and where to export or share files, or whether to email support.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter and the records it creates in its local app storage. This includes:

  • Stock labels, materials, shelf locations, usable dimensions and material costs.
  • Job titles, customer text, job dates, notes, glass sizes, quantities, allowances and rotation choices.
  • Cut plans, stock reservations, cut history and related stock snapshots.
  • Your workshop business name and measurement preferences.

This information is used on the device to provide inventory, planning, reporting and backup features. TintStash does not upload it to a developer-operated service. No sign-in with the developer is required.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library or precise device location. A stock “location” is text you enter, such as a shelf label, not GPS data.

2. Apple purchases

The optional Full Workshop unlock is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app reads Apple-verified transaction and entitlement information on the device to determine whether Full Workshop is unlocked. Loading a price, purchasing and restoring purchases can contact Apple.

TintStash does not receive your payment-card number. Apple's processing of account, payment and transaction information is governed by Apple's privacy practices. Restoring a purchase restores access to Full Workshop; it does not transfer workshop records.

3. Exports, backups and sharing

You can export PDF cut sheets, CSV data and full .tintstash backups through the system share interface. You choose the recipient, app or storage provider. Any information in those files is shared with the destination you select, under that destination's practices. TintStash does not automatically send exports to the developer.

Exported files are not encrypted by TintStash. A full backup can include customer names, notes, stock, jobs, history and settings. Keep exported files in a place you control and share only the information you intend to disclose.

The app has no automatic cross-device sync. Device-level backups and storage services you enable are controlled by you, your device settings and the relevant provider; they are separate from a TintStash developer cloud service.

4. Retention and deletion

Local workshop records remain in the app's storage while you keep them. You can edit or delete supported records in the app, subject to reservation and cut-history checks. The app also keeps a previous local save for recovery, so an older copy of a record may remain in that recovery file until a later save replaces it.

Deleting the app removes its local app data through the operating system. Offloading the app is different and may retain its data. Export a backup first if you want to keep your workshop. Copies you have exported, sent to another person, or included in a device or cloud backup are not deleted by removing the app; manage those copies separately.

Restoring a .tintstash backup replaces the current workshop. The developer cannot retrieve local workshop records that you have not separately sent to support.

5. Support messages

If you choose to email support, the support inbox receives your email address and the contents and attachments you send. That information is used to address your request. Sending email involves your email provider and the support email provider; it is not an automatic data transfer from the app.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, customer details or a full workshop backup in an initial support request. For questions about a support message or to request deletion of information you sent, contact 2603462296@qq.com.

6. This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The page code contains no advertising, analytics scripts, contact form or tracking cookies. As the website host, GitHub may process ordinary request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to deliver and operate the site. That hosting activity is separate from the app's local workshop storage.

7. Policy updates and contact

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy or support questions about TintStash, email 2603462296@qq.com.

See the support page for backup, restore and purchase instructions.
