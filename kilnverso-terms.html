Subscription and purchase information

Free records. Optional Pro tools.

Updated 8 November 2026

Free access and Pro

Kilnverso is operated by Wei Ye, an individual developer. Core studio records and exports are available without Pro and without a piece-count limit. Pro currently includes tools such as side-by-side review, resumable weighing and workflow customization. Existing records and exports remain available when Pro access ends.

Kilnverso is a pottery recordkeeping tool with local storage and user-directed backup, export and recovery. It does not provide automatic cross-device studio sync or promise Watch functionality. It does not control kilns, verify glaze chemistry, assess food safety or provide tax advice. Timers and reminders are not safety systems.

Plans and availability

The planned US offers are:

  • Monthly Pro: US$1.99 per month, automatically renewing.
  • Annual Pro: US$9.99 per year, billed as one annual amount and automatically renewing.
  • Lifetime Pro: US$19.99 once, a non-consumable purchase, not a renewing subscription.

These prices describe the planned US offers, not a claim that purchases are currently available. The development version uses local test offers. Actual availability, currency and prices depend on the App Store storefront. Before a real purchase, review Apple's displayed product, full charge and billing period. No free trial, introductory discount or Family Sharing benefit is promised here.

Payment, renewal and management

Apple handles purchase confirmation and payment. Monthly and annual plans renew automatically unless canceled through Apple's subscription controls. Review Apple's displayed renewal information and cancel before the renewal you do not want. Opening or closing Manage subscriptions alone does not confirm cancellation or any plan change.

Use Manage subscriptions in Kilnverso or the subscription settings for the Apple Account that made the purchase. Use Restore purchase to ask Apple to reconcile existing purchases. Removing the app is not a subscription cancellation. Access is determined from Apple's verified entitlement information, including expiration and revocation.

Lifetime Pro

Lifetime Pro covers the current Pro features through a one-time non-consumable purchase while that purchase remains valid. It does not promise future new services, separate apps, cloud services, hardware, future feature tiers or an unlimited support term. Buying Lifetime Pro does not cancel an existing subscription. Manage that subscription separately with Apple to avoid an unwanted renewal.
