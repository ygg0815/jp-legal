Privacy policy

Your studio, backups and support messages.

Updated 8 November 2026

Operator and scope

Kilnverso is operated by Wei Ye, an individual developer. This policy describes the current local-storage version of Kilnverso and these support pages. Contact 2603462296@qq.com with privacy questions.

Studio information and photos

The app stores the studio information you enter on your device, including pieces, materials, firing notes, recipes, reminders, optional sales or cost details, photos and preferences. The current version has no in-app studio account or automatic cross-device studio synchronization.

You choose photos through the system photo picker. Using the camera requires device permission. Selected or captured photos are copied into the app's local photo storage. Camera access can be controlled in device settings.

The current app does not include an advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Apple may independently process App Store or device diagnostic information according to your settings and Apple's policies. Local storage does not mean that the device makes no network connections.

Exports, backups and recovery

You choose when to prepare CSV, PDF or full studio backup files and where to save or share them. Full backups include photos. Single-piece PDFs use reduced-resolution photos; whole-studio PDFs are text-only. Preparing a file does not automatically send it to the developer.

Kilnverso does not encrypt exports, full backups or rescue packages. Your chosen storage or sharing provider may receive files you save there. Protect those files and review the destination before sharing. Device backups depend on your operating-system and account settings.

The app keeps a previous metadata snapshot for recovery. Recently deleted records, earlier snapshots, recovery-original files and separately saved exports can retain older information. Moving a piece to Recently deleted does not promise secure erasure of its photos or all copies. Rescue packages can contain raw metadata and safely named direct photo files, including deleted-record, unreferenced and orphan photos.

You manage records in the app and copies saved outside it separately. Keep a verified backup before removing the app or changing installations. Support cannot recover your studio simply by restoring a purchase.

Optional notifications

Reminders use Apple's local notification system with your permission. The app schedules the next 60 future tasks when reminders are refreshed, using generic lock-screen wording. You can decline or disable notifications. Task records remain in the studio and full backups.

Apple purchases

The app uses StoreKit to request product information, process purchases and restore access. It checks verified purchase information, including product identity, subscription expiration and revocation, to determine Pro access. Apple handles payment; Kilnverso's purchase screen does not collect payment-card details. See Apple's privacy policy for Apple's processing.

Support email and this website

If you email support, the receiving mailbox receives your email address, message and attachments. Include only what is needed for your request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or an entire studio, backup or rescue archive by default. Preparing an export does not send it to this mailbox. Contact the same address about information you have sent to support, including a request to correct or delete it. Support correspondence and attachments are retained for 12 months after your issue is resolved, then deleted, except where retention is required by law.

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and contain no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or contact forms. Visiting them sends a request to GitHub; GitHub may process request information under its Privacy Statement. Opening Apple links or using an email service involves the policies of those services.
