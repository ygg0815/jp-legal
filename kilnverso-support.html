Protect your records

In Settings, use Backup and restore to create a full studio backup. Save it somewhere you control and verify the file before removing the app or changing devices. Full backups include photos. CSV and PDF exports are useful reports, not substitutes for a full backup. Manual saving to Files or iCloud Drive is not automatic studio synchronization.

If your studio will not open

Keep the app installed. The recovery screen can inspect a previous local snapshot and explain what restoring it would replace. The snapshot may be older than your latest changes. Recovery cannot guarantee restoration of every change or damaged photo.

Raw recovery copies and rescue packages can preserve information when ordinary recovery is unavailable. Rescue packages are unencrypted, can include deleted-record or orphan photos, and are not directly importable studio backups. Review their contents before sharing them. Do not repeatedly reinstall or delete files to try to recover your studio.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Restore purchase to ask Apple to reconcile existing purchases. Use Manage subscriptions to open Apple's controls. Opening or closing these controls does not confirm cancellation or a plan change. If they cannot load, try again later or use your Apple Account subscription settings. If Apple's controls appear cut off, try turning your device upright.

Monthly and annual plans renew unless canceled. A lifetime purchase does not cancel another subscription. Lifetime covers current Pro features, without a promise of future new services. Existing records and exports remain available without Pro. See the subscription and purchase information for plan details and current availability.

For Apple-billed purchases, follow Apple's refund request guidance. Some purchases may be eligible; a refund is not guaranteed. Cancellation and refund requests are separate.

Reminders and safety

Notifications are optional. Permission, Focus and other device settings can affect delivery. Check due tasks in the app rather than relying on a notification. Kilnverso does not control a kiln and is not a safety alarm, firing recommendation or food-safety assessment.
