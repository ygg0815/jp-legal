Start with stock, then plan a job

  1. In Stock, tap + and enter each roll or rectangular remnant with a unique label, material, usable dimensions and shelf location. Match these labels to the physical pieces.
  2. In Jobs, tap +, choose the material and enter glass sizes, quantities, edge allowances, cut gaps and rotation choices.
  3. Choose Find a layout. Review dimensions and any panes that do not fit. A complete plan can be saved with Reserve materials.
  4. Use Confirm cut only when ready to record the physical cut. This updates stock once and creates records for retained rectangular offcuts. Re-measure and label the offcuts.

Layouts are estimates produced by heuristics, not guaranteed optimal. Check physical measurements, film direction, defects and installation allowances. A PDF cut sheet is a diagram, not a scale template.

Common questions

Why is some stock unavailable?

The stock must match the job’s material and have enough unreserved usable space. Other jobs may already reserve part of a roll. Check labels, dimensions, material names and outstanding reservations. Release a reservation before changing a reserved job.

Why can’t I undo a cut?

TintStash checks whether later work, edits or deletions depend on the affected stock or remnants. It blocks an undo that would make your records inconsistent. Read the error message and check the related jobs and cut history.

Where are my records?

Workshop records are saved in the app’s storage on this device. There is no developer cloud account or automatic cross-device sync. Deleting the app can remove its local records. Export a backup before reinstalling or changing devices.

How do I back up or move my workshop?

In Settings → Portable backup, choose Export full backup and save the .tintstash file to a location you control. On the destination device, use Restore from backup.

Restoring replaces the entire current workshop. Export the current workshop first if you need to keep it. Backups are unencrypted and can contain customer names and job notes. Protect any exported copies.

Can a CSV or PDF restore my records?

No. CSV is for checking stock and audit data in a spreadsheet; PDF is for viewing or sharing cut sheets. Use a full .tintstash backup to restore the workshop.

How do measurements and costs work?

Settings supports inches or millimeters. Changing the display unit does not change stored dimensions. Costs are recorded in USD without currency conversion. “Film drawn” represents raw film drawn from stock, including material that may be kept as remnants; it is not final job cost or profit.

Full Workshop purchases

Is Full Workshop a subscription?

No. Full Workshop is an optional one-time, non-consumable purchase through Apple. The price shown by the App Store applies in your region. The free workshop supports 3 saved jobs and 10 manually entered stock pieces; automatically created offcuts do not count toward that stock allowance.

The purchase removes those free-tier capacity limits. Existing records, planning, cut sheets, CSV export, and backup/restore remain available without it. Technical limits still apply: 2,000 stock pieces, 5,000 jobs and 7 MB of local records.

I purchased it, but it is still locked

Open Settings → Workshop access → Unlock Full Workshop (or Manage Full Workshop) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase and check your network connection. A pending purchase must be approved before access is unlocked.

Restoring a purchase restores access, not your stock or job records. Transfer those with a workshop backup.

The App Store cannot load the price

Check your connection and try Retry App Store later. Your existing workshop records are unchanged, and offline stock and planning features remain usable.
