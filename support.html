Purchases

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by the app. Open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device, choose Japanese Kana & Words, and tap Cancel. You keep Pro access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but Pro is still locked

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. If you are on a different Apple ID than the one you purchased with, sign in with that account first. Then relaunch the app.

What is the difference between Yearly and Lifetime?

Both unlock the same Pro features. Yearly renews automatically and starts with a 7-day free trial; Lifetime is a single payment with no renewal. If you buy Lifetime while a yearly subscription is active, cancel the subscription afterwards — Pro stays unlocked.

Audio and pronunciation

I hear nothing when I tap the speaker

  • Check the silent switch on the side of your iPhone and turn the volume up.
  • If you are on a call or connected to a Bluetooth device, audio may be routed elsewhere.
  • Pronunciation playback prefers high-quality cloud audio and falls back to the system voice when offline. If the fallback voice is missing, install a Japanese voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

The microphone does not start

Allow microphone access in Settings → Japanese Kana & Words → Microphone. Scoring needs a moment of quiet and a clear reading of the whole word — if no speech is detected, the app will not send anything to the scoring service and no daily check is used.

The AI tutor

What are the daily limits?

Free: 3 questions a day. Pro: 20 questions a day. The counter resets at midnight local time. Pronunciation scoring is separate: 5 checks a day free, 30 with Pro.

Why did it refuse my question?

The tutor is limited to Japanese learning — grammar, vocabulary, readings and why an answer was wrong. Off-topic requests are declined. If an answer looks wrong or inappropriate, use the report button under the reply; we review every report.

Your data

Where is my progress stored?

On your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. Progress is removed if you delete the app, so export a backup first if you plan to switch devices.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

In the Profile tab, open Data Backup and tap Export Progress; save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new device, install the app and use Import Backup on the same screen.

How do I switch between English and Chinese?

In the Profile tab, open Display & Appearance and change Language. The default follows your system language; the choice is remembered after that.
