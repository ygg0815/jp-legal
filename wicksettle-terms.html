1. Product purpose and limits

Wicksettle is a device-local production ledger for candle makers. It records material lots, actual batch inputs, yield, stock adjustments and cost snapshots. It is not a tax or accounting service, sales or invoicing system, safety certification, burn-test system or automatic recipe recommendation.

Figures depend on your entries. Check material identity, units, quantities, costs and output before settling a batch. Use actual measured mass rather than a container's advertised volume. Follow supplier limits, test each recipe and comply with applicable labeling and product-safety requirements.

Each ledger uses one currency, fixed after its first record, without currency conversion. Recovered mixture is recorded for reconciliation but does not return to original raw-material lots or reduce the batch cost. Missing labor, taxes and other expenses are not inferred. Cost per good unit is undefined when there are no good units.

2. Records and corrections

Drafts do not reserve or deduct materials. Confirming settlement deducts the recorded quantities once and freezes a cost snapshot. Voiding an incorrect settled record restores those quantities and retains the original record and restoration history; it does not physically restore materials.

Review your records and use corrections that reflect what actually happened. A failed pour is still a production event; voiding is intended for an incorrect record. The app does not independently verify measurements, inventory or legal compliance.

3. Free access and optional purchase

The free studio allows up to 5 currently settled, non-voided batches, including imported records. Drafts, materials, viewing existing records, reports, exports, backup import and corrections remain available. Voided batches and the example workspace do not count toward the free limit.

Full Studio Unlock removes the settlement limit through one non-consumable in-app purchase. It is not a subscription. The current price, currency and applicable purchase terms are shown by Apple before purchase. No promise about every future feature or service is made by this page.

Apple processes purchases and restoration. Pricing, purchase and restoration require internet access and may require Apple Account authentication. Restore using the Apple Account that purchased the unlock. A restored entitlement does not restore ledger records. The app uses Apple's verified on-device transaction history for access; reconnect and restore if a purchase is not recognized.

If Apple reports a refund or revocation, access returns to the free settlement rule without deleting or blocking reading of existing ledger records. Pending or unverified transactions do not grant the unlock. Billing and refund requests are handled through Apple's purchase support, subject to Apple's rules and applicable law.

4. Your data and backups

There is no developer-operated ledger account or automatic cloud sync. Keep regular full JSON backups, especially before deleting the app, replacing a device or restoring an older ledger. The previous-version copy and recovery archives are local safeguards, not off-device backups.

Importing a validated backup replaces the ledger rather than merging it. CSV reports cannot restore it. Exported files are not encrypted by Wicksettle and may include business, supplier and other private information. Store and share them carefully and enter only information you are entitled to use. See the privacy policy for data practices.

The app has technical validation and storage limits, including a 25 MB ledger and backup limit. A purchase does not remove these technical limits.
