Privacy policy

Thai Path does not require an app account and has no advertising, analytics or tracking. The developer does not collect personal data through the app. Progress, settings and custom cards are stored on your device. You can reset learning progress and export or import a backup. You choose where to save or share any exported file.

Speaking practice uses the microphone only when you start recording after granting permission. Recordings stay on your device and are deleted when you leave the practice screen. Study reminders are optional local notifications. You can change permissions in iOS Settings. Bundled Thai audio plays offline. If you use an iOS system voice, Apple may need to download it.

Apple handles App Store downloads and payments. The app checks verified StoreKit transactions to unlock the full edition and does not receive your payment details. Apple services are subject to the Apple Privacy Policy. If you email support, the developer uses the email address and information you choose to provide to respond.

Terms of use

The Apple Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement applies to the app. Thai Path provides Thai-language learning materials. See Profile → Privacy & content for credits and licenses for third-party vocabulary. These app-specific notices supplement the Apple license and preserve your rights under applicable consumer law.

Purchases and refunds

Thai Path is free to download and includes foundational lessons and sample cards. The full edition unlocks the complete word and sentence libraries through a one-time, non-consumable App Store purchase. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. The app shows the price for your App Store before you confirm. If Apple requires purchase approval, access unlocks after the transaction is approved and verified.

To restore access, use the Apple Account that made the purchase and open Profile → Full edition → Restore purchases. Restoring access does not import learning progress. To request a refund, follow Apple’s refund instructions or visit Report a Problem. Apple processes refund requests. If the purchase is refunded, full-edition access may be revoked and saved progress remains. Your statutory consumer rights apply.

Support

Email 2603462296@qq.com for app help, privacy inquiries or purchase restoration issues. For a technical issue, include the app and iOS versions and the steps to reproduce it.
