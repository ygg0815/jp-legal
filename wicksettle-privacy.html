Privacy and your data

Privacy, explained.

How Wicksettle handles local records, Apple purchases, exports and support messages.

Updated November 7, 2026 · Wicksettle 1.0

Your ledger stays on your device

Wicksettle's ledger features do not require a developer account and do not send your material, production or cost records to a developer-operated server. The app includes no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Apple handles App Store purchases and purchase restoration. Exporting files, opening external links or contacting support can send information to the destination you choose, as described below.

1. Information stored locally

The app stores the information you enter: material names and categories, supplier references and lot codes, purchase dates, quantities and costs; batch names, recipe references, production dates, planned and actual output, material consumption, poured and recovered weights, additional costs and notes; stock adjustments and reasons; settled cost snapshots, void history and your ledger currency.

These records are stored in the app's private storage. The app keeps a previous saved ledger and recovery archives when a ledger is replaced or recovered. The optional example workspace is separate and does not save example edits into your real ledger.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo-library access. Files are selected or saved through Apple's system file interface.

2. Apple purchases and verification

The optional Full Studio Unlock uses Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes the purchase, payment and restoration under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The app receives transaction and entitlement information from Apple and verifies it on the device to determine access, including refunds or revocations. It does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password.

There is no developer-operated purchase server, transaction upload or separate sign-in. Purchase access is derived from Apple's verified transaction history, not from a flag in your exported ledger. Price requests, purchases and explicit purchase restoration use Apple's network services; restoration may prompt for Apple Account authentication. The app also reads on-device entitlement history on launch and when it becomes active.

Apple may independently process App Store or device diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies. This is separate from Wicksettle's local ledger storage.

3. Exports, backups and external services

You choose when to export a full JSON backup or CSV batch report and where to save or share it. These files may contain supplier details, business information and anything entered in notes. Wicksettle does not encrypt exported files or upload them automatically. Protect them and share only what you intend.

The storage or sharing service you select processes the files under its own terms and privacy policy. Apple device backups may include local app data depending on your device settings. This is separate from automatic app-to-app cloud sync, which Wicksettle does not provide.

Opening support, privacy or license links makes a web request to that website. These public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information such as IP address and browser details under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages have no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or contact forms.

4. Retention, correction and deletion

Local ledger data remains on your device until it is removed. Draft deletion, stock corrections and voiding a settled batch have different purposes: voiding preserves the original record and audit history, and older information can remain in local recovery copies. It is not an erasure request.

Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading an app may retain it. Export a full backup before reinstalling, resetting or replacing a device. Exported files, copies sent to others and device backups are separate and must be managed at those destinations. A purchase restore does not recover ledger records.

Because the developer does not receive your ledger through normal app use, support cannot retrieve or remotely delete it. You control the local files and any exports.

5. Support messages

If you email 2603462296@qq.com, the developer receives the address, message and attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond to your request. Email providers also process that communication under their own policies.

Include only information needed to explain the issue. Remove confidential supplier, customer and financial details from screenshots or sample files. Contact the same address for questions about this policy or information you have sent to support.

6. Policy updates

This policy describes Wicksettle 1.0. If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date. For help using the app, visit Wicksettle support.
