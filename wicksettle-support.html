Start with one supplier lot

  1. Choose your currency. Before adding records, open Settings → Studio setup. The ledger uses one currency and locks it once records exist. Changing device region does not convert or relabel historical costs.
  2. Receive materials. Add wax, fragrance, additives and counted components in Materials. Keep separate deliveries and supplier lots separate. Enter the total purchase cost for the received quantity.
  3. Record a batch. Start a batch in Studio or Batches. Add each material lot actually used, including more than one lot of the same ingredient when needed. Saving a draft does not reserve or deduct stock.
  4. Reconcile and settle. Enter good and rejected units, poured and recovered mixture weights, and additional costs. Review the quantities, then confirm settlement to deduct materials once and freeze the cost snapshot.

To try the workflow without changing your real ledger, choose Explore example workspace in Settings. Example changes stay separate.

Understand your numbers

  • Internal mass is measured in grams. Purchase entry also accepts mass ounces, not fluid ounces. Batch weights use grams.
  • Poured mass includes mixture in good and rejected candles, excluding containers and wicks. Recovered mixture is recorded separately. It is not returned to the original material lots or credited against cost.
  • Cost per good candle includes consumed materials and entered additional costs, including the cost of rejects and process loss. With zero good candles, the per-unit cost is undefined.
  • The app does not infer missing labor, tax, sales or profit. A recipe reference is a label, not an automatically validated candle formula.

Wicksettle is a recordkeeping tool. Follow supplier limits, test your candles, and follow applicable labeling and product-safety requirements.

Correct a record

For a stock-count correction or spill, record a counted-stock adjustment with a reason. It does not rewrite the original purchase basis or settled cost snapshots.

To correct an incorrectly settled record, use Void & restore stock with a reason, then create the correct batch. The original record and stock-restoration entries stay in history. Do not void an actual failed pour just to hide the loss; record its actual output instead.

Back up before replacing a device

In Settings, choose Export full backup and save the JSON file somewhere outside this device. It contains the full ledger, including materials, drafts, settled and voided batches, cost snapshots and adjustment history. The file is not encrypted by Wicksettle; protect it.

Restore from backup… validates a selected JSON backup and asks before replacing the entire current ledger. It does not merge records. The app also keeps a previous saved version and local recovery archives, but these do not protect against device loss or app deletion.

Export batch summary (CSV) creates a readable report, not a restorable backup. CSV column names follow the app language; numbers use a decimal point and dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Backup files and ledgers have a 25 MB size limit.

If the app cannot verify a saved ledger, it protects the file rather than silently replacing it. Use Settings to restore a known-good full backup or explicitly recover the previous saved version. Do not delete the app before preserving your records.

Free studio, purchase and restore

The free studio allows up to 5 currently settled, non-voided batches. Imported settled batches count too. Materials, drafts, existing records, reports, exports, backup import and corrections remain available. Voided batches and the separate example workspace do not count toward the free limit.

Full Studio Unlock is an optional, non-consumable, one-time purchase that removes the settlement limit. It is not a subscription. The App Store shows the current price and currency before purchase; technical storage and validation limits still apply.

Open the full-studio offer with View one-time unlock. To recover an earlier purchase, choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that bought it. Apple handles payment and restoration. Loading a current price, buying or restoring requires an internet connection and may require Apple Account sign-in.

Ledger work is local and can be used offline. The app checks Apple's verified on-device purchase history for unlock access, including offline when that history is available. If an earlier purchase is not recognized after reinstalling or changing devices, reconnect and restore it. Restoring a purchase does not restore your ledger.

If Apple revokes or refunds the unlock and that change reaches the app, the free settlement rule applies again. Existing records are not deleted and remain readable, exportable and available for correction. A pending purchase unlocks only after Apple confirms it. For billing or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support; Apple determines eligibility.

Languages and devices

Wicksettle supports iPhone and iPad on iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. The interface is available in English, Traditional Chinese and Japanese. In Settings, use Change app language to open the app's iOS settings. Number and date formatting follows your device region.

There is no developer account, team sharing or automatic cloud synchronization. Read the privacy policy and license and product information.
