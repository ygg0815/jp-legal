1. What TintStash does

TintStash is a single-device workshop tool for flat window-film rolls, rectangular remnants and rectangular glass cuts. It records stock and jobs, compares possible layouts, reserves materials, records confirmed cuts, and exports cut sheets and workshop data.

It is a planning and record-keeping aid. It does not inspect physical film, measure glass, perform a cut, or determine whether an installation meets a customer's specifications or local requirements.

2. Check the plan before cutting

  • Verify all dimensions, units, quantities, stock labels, material specifications and available stock against the physical job.
  • Check film direction, defects, rotation restrictions, trim allowances, cutting gaps and installation requirements before reserving or confirming a cut.
  • Layouts use heuristics and are not guaranteed to be globally optimal. A layout does not guarantee a particular material saving or job outcome.
  • PDF cut sheets are diagrams, not scale templates. Re-measure and label any physical remnants you keep.
  • Reservations do not physically separate stock. Confirm a cut in the app only when you intend to record that stock change. Undo may be blocked when later records depend on the affected material.

Costs in version 1.0 are recorded in USD without currency conversion. “Film drawn” is raw film taken from stock, including material that may be retained as remnants. It is not a final job cost, customer quotation or profit calculation.

3. Free workshop and optional purchase

The free workshop supports 3 saved jobs and 10 manually entered stock pieces at a time. Automatically created cut offcuts do not count toward that stock allowance.

Full Workshop is an optional one-time, non-consumable purchase through Apple, not a subscription. It removes the free-tier job and manual-stock capacity limits. The price and currency displayed by the App Store apply to the transaction you confirm.

Existing records, planning, PDF cut sheets, CSV export, and backup/restore remain available without the unlock. Technical limits still apply, including 2,000 stock pieces, 5,000 jobs and 7 MB of local records in version 1.0.

Purchases and restores depend on Apple account verification and may require a network connection. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restoring a purchase restores access, not workshop records. If Apple revokes or refunds a purchase, the unlock may no longer be available; existing workshop records are not deleted by that entitlement change.

Apple handles App Store billing. For a billing question or refund request, use Apple's purchase support. Refund eligibility is determined by Apple's process and applicable law; this page does not promise a refund outcome.

4. Local records and backups

Workshop records are stored on your device. TintStash has no developer cloud account or automatic cross-device sync. Deleting the app can remove its local records.

Export regular full .tintstash backups to a location you control, especially before reinstalling, changing devices or restoring a different workshop. Restoring a backup replaces the entire current workshop. CSV and PDF exports cannot restore the workshop.

Exports are not encrypted by TintStash and may contain customer names, notes or other business information you entered. Protect those files and check their contents before sharing. Use customer information only when you have the right to do so. See the privacy policy for data handling, retention and deletion details.
